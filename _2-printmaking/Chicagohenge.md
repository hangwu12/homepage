<h4 style = "text-align: center;"> <i> Chicagohenge</i></h4>
<p style = "text-align: center;">A printmaking work on the city Chicago </p>
During the weeks surrounding the March and September equinoxes, a magical event called Chicagohenge transforms the city's canyons. The setting (or rising) sun aligns directly with the east-west streets, flooding the skyscraper-lined corridors with brilliant light and offering a breathtaking view for those positioned on streets like Madison, Randolph, or Kinzie.
